Tools

EfficStudio

The video workbench behind our channel · 我们频道背后的视频工作台

EfficStudio is the in-house workbench we use to make the explainer videos of the EfficLab中文 channel, from first idea to published video.

EfficStudio 是我们自研的视频工作台，EfficLab中文 频道的讲解视频从选题到发布都在这里完成。

Made with care · 认真做内容

Every fact is checked before it goes on screen, and every word is spoken in the host's own voice.

每条事实都核对过，每句旁白都是频道主本人录的。

Motion graphics · 动态画面

Animated diagrams and 3D scenes designed for each topic, so technical ideas are easy to see.

为每个主题专门设计的动画图解和 3D 场景，让技术原理一眼看懂。

Our own channels · 自有频道

Finished videos go to our own Bilibili and YouTube channels, checked privately before they go public.

成片发布到我们自己的 B站 和 YouTube，先私有核对再公开。

EfficStudio runs only on our own computer and uploads only to our own channels; it is not a public service and has no other users. · 它只在我们自己的电脑上运行、只发布到我们自己的频道，不对外提供服务，也没有其他用户。

Google / YouTube

EfficStudio asks for one Google permission only, youtube.upload, to upload our own videos and set their thumbnails on our own channel. It does not read, change or delete anything else on the account.

只申请 youtube.upload 一项权限，用来上传我们自己的视频、设置缩略图；不读取、不修改、不删除账号里的其他任何内容。
