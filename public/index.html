EfficStudio

The video workbench behind our channel · 我们频道背后的视频工作台

Every EfficLab中文 explainer is written as code: each film is a web page that can draw any moment of itself, rendered frame by frame into a 1080p video. An AI agent does the research, the script and the picture; code does every step that has one right answer; and the host confirms each step before the next one starts.

EfficLab中文 的每一部讲解视频都是用代码写出来的：一部片子就是一个能画出任意时刻的网页，逐帧渲染成 1080p 视频。AI agent 负责调研、解说词和画面，结果唯一的步骤交给代码，每一步都由频道主确认后才进入下一步。

  1. 1Research & outline调研 · 大纲
  2. 2Script解说词
  3. 3Picture画面
  4. 4Fact-check联网校对
  5. 5Recording真人录音
  6. 6HD & publish成片 · 发布

Harness · 渲染底座

Headless Chrome captures renderAt(t) frame by frame, ffmpeg muxes the soundtrack. Every visual beat is tied to the phrase that's spoken, so the picture re-times itself to any narration.

逐帧截图 + 混音合成；画面跟着旁白的每个短语走，换一版录音，画面自动重新对齐。

Film kit & styles · 片子套件

A shared frame (title card, captions, outro, covers), 15 motion-design styles, and a hardware-3D pipeline: three.js scenes baked into Blender for the final render.

共用的片头、字幕、片尾和封面；15 种动态设计风格；硬件题材走 three.js → Blender 的 3D 管线。

Fact-checked · 事实校对

Every claim and every term is checked against primary sources before recording. Hardware sizes on screen come from published data, never estimates.

录音前逐条核对原始资料；屏幕上的硬件尺寸只用公开数据，不估算。

Recording booth · 录音台

The host's own voice only, recorded line by line in the browser and cut into per-line clips automatically, with an approved voice EQ.

只用频道主本人的声音，网页里逐句录，自动切分、对齐。

Edit by hand · 所见即所改

Click any element in the preview to change its position, text or timing; split long captions; adjust covers by dragging. Automatic checks for layout, loudness, true peak, black frames and flicker.

点选画面元素直接改位置、文字和时间；封面拖拽调整；排版、响度、黑帧、闪烁自动检查。

Publish flow · 发布流

Preview exactly what goes out (title, description, tags, cover), then upload to Bilibili and YouTube. Hash-locked to the preview, private by default.

预览什么就上传什么（内容变了要重新预览），默认私有发布，核对无误再公开。

EfficStudio runs only on our own computer and uploads only to our own channels; it is not a public service and has no other users. · 它只在我们自己的电脑上运行、只发布到我们自己的频道，不对外提供服务，也没有其他用户。

Google / YouTube

EfficStudio asks for one Google permission only, youtube.upload, to upload our own videos and set their thumbnails on our own channel. It does not read, change or delete anything else on the account.

只申请 youtube.upload 一项权限，用来上传我们自己的视频、设置缩略图；不读取、不修改、不删除账号里的其他任何内容。
